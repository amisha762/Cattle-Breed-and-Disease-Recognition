🧠 About the Model
Deep Learning Computer Vision High Accuracy

This system uses a Deep Learning-based image classification model trained on thousands of cattle images to identify 41 breeds and 4 diseases from a single photograph.

Detects 4 common cattle diseases — Foot & Mouth Disease, Lumpy Skin Disease, Mastitis, and Bovine Respiratory Disease.
